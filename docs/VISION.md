# Bluefin: what it's meant to be

Your words, kept here so every decision gets checked against them.

## The feel

- **A friendslop game.** Something you mess around in with friends (and maybe put on Steam). Lethal Company's
  shape: a quota to make, stakes, a crew yelling at each other; a GTA heist's: a plan, roles, the big score.
- **Funny. Very funny.** Stupid stuff on purpose. Failing should be a joke you want to see, not a game over.
- **Sea of Thieves sailing:** it can take a while (finding the tuna is its own mission; the fight can be long),
  as long as it's fun while you're doing it: everyone's hands busy, the horizon promising something, the boat a toy,
  things that can go wrong.
- **Realistic where it counts:** real tuna fishing (birds, trolling, the fight, the harpoon, bleeding and icing),
  no nerfs for being alone. Creepy only from the sea and the weather.
- **Polish before features:** the base idea is in. Make it beautiful (animation, visuals, feel, sound) and make sure
  the player always knows what's going on, before adding anything new.

## Chekhov's gun: everything there has a use

If something big enough to look usable is in the world, you can use it, or it goes. A chair you can't sit in
looks broken. "It's funny" or "it's chill" is reason enough (sitting in a chair to watch the sunset counts). Small
set dressing (a can, a bobblehead, a coil of rope, a buoy) is fine as decoration. If the boat would look wrong
without the thing, make it usable; don't keep it as a prop.

## Teaching: shown, not read

- **Dale from the bank** (First Coastal Savings & Loan's loan officer) on the boat's VHF: you're his investment, so
  he calls in the first time each thing happens, tells you what to do, and isn't nice about it (`ui/tips.gd`).
- **The owner's manual** in the Esc menu: everything, in the voice of a manual nobody proofread (`ui/manual.gd`).
- Later, maybe: a cheesy 90s training tape ("So You Bought a Tuna Boat") on a TV in the dock shack, skippable.

## Ideas to come back to (parked, not built)

- **Losing the boat means a crap job.** Instead of a game over when the bank takes the boat (or you're broke), the
  crew clocks in at a parody supermarket or coffee shop (made-up brands, not real ones) for a short, dumb shift
  (bagging groceries, burning lattes) to earn enough for another go.
- Overboard and the life ring; night danger; a storm rolling in; the giant; something in the fog.
- Sea life to spot (whales blowing, dolphins, sharks); the horn, the radio; sharks going for a hooked fish.
- VS mode: two boats on the same sea, racing for the same birds.
- Proximity voice chat.
