# Making the crew on Meshy's free plan (by hand, about 10 minutes)

Meshy's free plan: 100 credits a month, no card, rigging included, no API (so this is done on the website).
Free models are CC BY 4.0: fine to use, credit "Models made with Meshy (meshy.ai)" in the game's credits.

## Steps, for each model below

1. Sign up at meshy.ai (free). In the left sidebar click **Model**, then the **Text to 3D** tab at the top of
   the panel. Pick **High Detail** with **Meshy 6 Lite** (the one the free plan can download).
2. Paste the **Prompt** into the Prompt box. Under **Pose** pick **A-Pose** (people only). Click **Generate**
   (about a minute). It comes out grey: that's normal.
3. Texture it with the texture tool in the toolbar over the model: paste the **Texture prompt**, generate.
4. People only: **Rig** it (Animate / Rigging), height as given. Keep the walk and run if it offers them.
5. **Download as GLB** and save it as `art/generated/<name>/<name>.glb` in this repo (the rigged one if you
   rigged it). Tell me, and I take it from there: clean it, fit it, wire it into the game, screenshot it.

**For now, only crew_yellow and the bluefin.** Everyone plays the same fisherman for now; the colour of each
player's oilskins is changed in game. The other three people below are kept for later (body types for
customization), not needed now.

a cartoon, not chibi, no oversized head. Standing in an A-pose, facing forward, full body, feet flat. A weathered
stocky fisherman in his fifties with a barrel chest and a round belly. Long face, heavy brow, big broken nose, short
grey-brown scruffy beard. Bright yellow oilskin bib-and-brace fishing trousers with braces over a navy wool
fisherman's jumper with the sleeves pushed up, a red knit watch cap, orange rubber gloves, dark green rubber deck
boots, a knife on his belt.

**Texture prompt:**
Hand-painted look: soft painted light and shadow following the forms, cool purple-grey in the shadows, warm ruddy
skin, clean smooth surfaces with a slight sheen, no photographic noise or pores. Lived-in wear: scuffs on the boots,
a few fish-blood smudges and salt marks on the oilskins. Bright yellow oilskins, navy jumper, red knit cap, orange
gloves, dark green boots.
