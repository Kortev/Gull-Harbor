# Voice lines

Everything said over the VHF goes through the game's radio (ui/radio.gd). It cuts the lows and highs, adds some
crunch, squashes it flat, and opens and closes each call with the squelch and a roger beep. **So record it clean
and dry:** no filters, no reverb.

## How to record

- Any mic is fine (a phone works). Hold it close, a hand's width away, in a quiet room: a closet full of clothes is
  great.
- **Talk like you're on a radio:** a little louder and faster than normal, clipped, as if you're holding a button
  down. Don't do a radio voice: the game does the radio part.
- One file per line, a beat of silence before and after is fine (it's trimmed by ear, not needed).
- Save as **.ogg or .wav** with the file name below, in `audio/voice/dale/`. Restart the game and it's used: the
  card stays up as long as the clip.
- Say roughly the words on the card. They don't have to match exactly, but close is better, since the words are
  on screen while you say them. If you'd rather ad-lib a line, tell me and I'll change the card to match.

## Dale, from the bank (`audio/voice/dale/`)

Dale is the loan officer at First Coastal Savings & Loan. You're his investment. He's friendly, a bit pushy, and
a bit nervous about his money: a car salesman who's been put in charge of a fishing fleet.

| File | When | Line |
|---|---|---|
| `out` | heading out, first time | Dale, from the bank. Tuna feed under birds. Find a flock. |
| `cast` | out a while, no baits in the water | Get some baits out. Pick up a rod, hold the click, let go. |
| `flock` | first flock you look at | Diving birds, white water? Bluefin. Go. Birds sitting? Wait for them. |
| `fast` | running up on birds too fast | Ease her down to troll: nothing bites at speed. Get out ahead of them, drag your baits across their nose. |
| `bite` | first bite, rod in its holder | That's a reel screaming. Grab the rod! |
| `fight` | your first fight | Reel screaming? Hands off. Grinding? Pump: lift slow, wind as you drop. He jumps, drop the tip. |
| `spiral` | first death spiral | Colour! Wind while he comes up, ease off while he goes under. |
| `alone` | fighting with nobody else aboard | Alone? Rod in the holder, Q. Steer, then grab it again. |
| `captain` | at the wheel with a fish on | Captain: stern to the fish. S backs down. Under the boat? Hands off the throttle. |
| `lines` | other baits still out in a fight | Reel the other lines in before they tangle. |
| `alongside` | first fish alongside, the skiff | Harpoon off the port rack, then haul it in. Ninety seconds. |
| `alongside_wire` | first fish alongside, the sportfisher | Wire it at the door, harpoon it off the port rack, haul. Ninety seconds. |
| `deck` | first fish on deck | Bleed it, ice it. Quick. A cold fish pays double. |
| `stale` | a bait washed out | That bait's washed out. Bait in hand, E on the rod, cast it again. |
| `wait` | out a long while with nothing | Slow? Chum off the transom brings them to you. |
| `rivals` | Rivals, the countdown at the dock | Rivals: most pounds aboard at dusk wins the day, first to two days. Both boats go on GO. |
| `drive` | the first time out (or the Rivals countdown) | Wheel's on the console. E to take it, W and S throttle, A and D steer. |
| `radar` | the first flock painting on the radar | On the radar, birds are the speck that stays put sweep after sweep. Closer, it goes orange. Get ahead of it. |
| `rival` | Rivals, the other boat close | That's them. Cross their stern and you'll part their lines. The horn's on the dash: E. |
| `dusk` | Rivals, the light going | Light's going. Whatever's aboard at dusk is what they weigh. |

More voices (the other boats' skippers chattering on 16, the fish buyer at the dock) come later, and they'll be
listed here as they're added.
